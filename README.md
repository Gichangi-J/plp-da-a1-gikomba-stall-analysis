## Business problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded her daily sales for two weeks alongside a rough record of her stock levels. She needs to understand how the business is performing, whether sales are growing, which days perform best, what products need restocking, and where she should focus her time and resources. This analysis uses core Python to calculate sales performance, compare weekdays with weekends, classify sales days, identify products at or below their reorder levels, and calculate each product's gross margin.

## Top three findings

1. **Sales increased by 10.66%** from KES 22,050 in week 1 to KES 24,400 in week 2.
2. **Weekend average sales were 82.50% higher** than weekday average sales: KES 4,900 compared with KES 2,685.
3. **Tomatoes and sukuma need restocking:** tomatoes had 18 kg against a 25 kg reorder level, and sukuma had 9 kg against a 15 kg reorder level.
